DanceIR

Supplementary Videos

This page provides the supplementary video material for the paper Estimating Global Tempo from Dance Movement Using Kinematic Rhythmic Anchors.

For each dance genre, four supplementary videos are included, each annotated with the estimated BPM, the reference BPM and the corresponding Body part that reflects musical tempo:

  1. Rhythmic Anchor Pulse Only – shows the movement-derived rhythmic anchor pulse, rendered as audio clicks.
  2. Music + Anchor Pulse – shows the original music track with the same audio-click pulse overlaid.
